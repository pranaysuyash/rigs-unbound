# Physics Lab 01: replaceable chassis and wheel dynamics

- Date: 2026-07-25
- Status: implemented and locally accepted
- Decision: [ADR-0017](../decisions/ADR-0017-replaceable-dynamics-foundation.md)
- Risk class: medium local runtime and dependency change
- Product position: disposable evidence fixture, not a game mode or product identity

## Objective

Execute the first bounded slice of the physics exploration map:

> TypeScript + Vite + Three.js + Rapier 3D, with simulation isolated behind a
> project-owned interface.

The slice must prove a dynamic chassis, raycast wheels, surface-dependent
response, fixed-step ownership, view-policy reuse, reset/recovery, and browser
telemetry without transferring rig identity, gameplay authority, input
ownership, persistence shape, or activity logic into Rapier.

## Product and portability contract

Rigs Unbound remains a broad vehicle universe. Physics Lab 01 is one diagnostic
fixture for the wheeled-controller family. It does not privilege tractors,
buggies, wheels, ground travel, racing, or this particular world.

The continuing portability question is:

> Can a bicycle, toy car, tractor, and spacecraft use the project-owned intent,
> state, camera, persistence, activity, and observability contracts while
> selecting bounded controller and solver adapters appropriate to their motion
> grammar?

The correct answer is allowed to include several controller families. It must
not become one universal controller with a growing set of vehicle flags.

## Delivered slice

1. Add normalized semantic vehicle intent independent of keyboard keys.
2. Add project-owned dynamics contracts containing no Rapier types or handles.
3. Implement Rapier as the first `DynamicsService`.
4. Build one dynamic chassis with four raycast wheels.
5. Use four versioned surface profiles: asphalt, gravel, mud, and ice.
6. Extract transforms, wheel contacts, suspension, slip, impulses, and metrics
   into plain project-owned telemetry.
7. Capture and restore the vehicle through plain data rather than solver
   handles.
8. Run a fixed-step accumulator with selectable 30, 60, and 120 Hz simulation.
9. Provide pause, reset, recovery, time-scale, and collider-debug controls.
10. Reuse the six camera policies: Chase, Hood, Side, Tactical, Top-down, and
    Survey.
11. Keep the vehicle front aligned with positive local Z and make that front
    visually unambiguous.
12. Expose stable browser hooks and an automated acceptance workflow.
13. Keep the Rapier WebAssembly payload out of Field 02's entry module.

## Adjacent-capability audit

The implementation is checked continuously against the architectural tests
from the Adjacent Activity Expansion discussion:

- input becomes semantic intent before reaching a controller;
- the solver is a bounded controller dependency, not a universal rig model;
- cameras remain policies selected through the shared camera vocabulary;
- world/gameplay code should query capability or mobility contracts, not rig
  names;
- renderer extraction receives project-owned state rather than solver bodies;
- capture/reset data contains no Rapier handles;
- telemetry records loading, render, contact, surface, and physics evidence;
- no repeated `activeRig === "tractor"` or `vehicle.type === "tractor"` branch
  is allowed across shared runtime layers.

The current pattern search found no such shared-runtime tractor-name branch.
The remaining explicit tractor access is in a tested legacy save migration,
where translating a historical tractor-only record is the bounded purpose.

## Explicit boundaries

- No claim that Rapier is the permanent or final solver.
- No claim that a raycast-wheel controller fits tracked, balance, hover,
  buoyancy, flight, or six-degree motion.
- No merger of Physics Lab state into the Field 02 save record.
- No public deployment in this slice.
- No authored production vehicle asset.
- No activity-specific rescue, combat, farming, or race logic in the solver.
- Trailer constraints and articulated excavator joints remain the next
  evidence-bearing comparison, not hidden scope in the chassis slice.
- Player feel and representative-device performance require external evidence.

## Review passes

### Pass 1 — immediate correctness

Check positive-throttle direction, steering, wheel contact, four surface
profiles, all camera modes, reset, frequency selection, narrow layout, and
browser-console health.

### Pass 2 — architecture and long-term viability

Check that semantic intent, dynamics contracts, extraction, cameras, and
surface data remain project-owned; Rapier types stay inside its adapter; Field
02 remains separately loadable; and no universal controller or
vehicle-name-gated gameplay path appears.

### Pass 3 — rule compliance and supervision readiness

Reconcile ADR, research map, plan, acceptance record, exploration map, README,
tools documentation, worklog, progress, live browser state, tests, build
evidence, local git state, and parallel-work preservation.

## Anything else?

Physics Lab 01 earns the right to attempt articulated vehicle fantasies; it
does not earn the right to make every rig a four-wheel chassis. The next solver
experiment should connect unstable cargo and a motorized tool to a real
capability such as towing, lifting, construction, rescue, or recovery.

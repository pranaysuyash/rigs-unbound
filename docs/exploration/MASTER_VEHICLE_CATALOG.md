---

## Catalog Overview & Classification Grammar

Every vehicle in **Rigs Unbound** is treated as a character with a distinct silhouette, locomotion class, repair history, and functional hardpoints.

### Vehicle Upgrade Tier Grammar
Every rig evolves across three distinct tiers:
1. **Tier 1 (v1: Found / Barn Find / Base):** Rusted, raw, minimal attachments, exposed wiring/linkages.
2. **Tier 2 (v2: Restored / Workhorse):** Reinforced chassis, repaired cab/body, secondary tool mounts, tuned powertrain.
3. **Tier 3 (v3: Specialized / Overcharged):** High-power specialized modules, extreme environmental armor, signature stance.

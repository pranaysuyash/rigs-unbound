## Architecture accepted

### Camera

- `src/game/camera.ts` owns rig-specific physical mounts only.
- `src/game/scene-query.ts` owns solver/renderer-independent nearest-hit
  evidence across terrain, procedural obstacles/felled memory, and typed
  authored structures.
- The renderer owns smoothing, safety margin, fallback framing, and
  self-intersection evidence without becoming spatial truth.

### Rig acquisition and persistence

- `src/game/rig-ids.ts` is the dependency-free identity vocabulary.
- `RIG_HOME_BERTHS` is authored world data reused by fresh state and exceptional
  recovery.
- Schema v6 writes a new key, reads v5 first, and selectively relocates only
  pristine inactive legacy Drift state. Any moved, used, damaged, modified,
  active, or attached state stays where the player left it.

### Traversability

- `src/game/terrain-traversal.ts` is one substrate refusal shared by ground and
  hover adapters.
- It sweeps the motion-direction leading support edge and checks the support
  rise across the footprint.
- It does not replace gearing/grip/hover feel on ordinary grades.
- Semantic reason `terrain-face` remains visible to player and operator.

### Player/developer UI

- Default URL is the player surface.
- `?surface=developer` and `?acceptance=field-02` expose diagnostics and lab
  navigation.
- Acceptance mutation hooks are query-gated, absent from player controls, and
  operate only on local deterministic state.
- Manual acceptance stepping prevents wall-clock animation frames from racing
  scripted fixtures.

## Evidence caveats

- The long-running trailer capture and browser daemon materially inflated
  navigation, screenshot, and performance timings during this run. Functional
  browser results are valid; these timings are not a representative-device
  performance baseline.
- Browser automation proves contract behavior and visible layout, not whether
  the new first-session labels are memorable or whether terrain-face refusal
  feels natural to external players.
- Direct Physics/Box3D lab implementations were not changed by this package;
  their dedicated acceptance scripts remain the canonical solver-lab evidence.

## Subassembly Node Contract
- `chassis-tubular-pod`: Compact sealed titanium cylindrical chassis body pod (0.65m total width)
- `6-wheeled-magnetic-drive`: 6 independently driven magnetic/stepper traction wheels for vertical pipe climbing
- `pantograph-camera-mast`: 360-degree pantograph elevating HD optical sensor head
- `dual-led-spotlights`: Twin high-lumens LED inspection spotlights with optical lenses
- `tether-spool-connector`: Rear strain-relieved fiber optic control tether connector spool
